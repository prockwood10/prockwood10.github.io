Spotlight

Tandem
Olive Oil

The olive trees around Napa Valley's vineyards are mostly decorative. Every fall the fruit ripens, drops, and rots. In 2017, a lifelong friend and I saw free fruit hanging over some of the most expensive agricultural land in America and started harvesting it.

Tandem ran for eight seasons: olives picked by hand, pressed into premium extra virgin oil, and sold back through the very wineries whose trees we picked. It taught me to sell before you build, and that margins are made in operations. I wound it down in 2025 to go all-in on Stanford.
